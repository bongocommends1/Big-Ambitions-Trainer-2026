## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is a role-playing business and life simulation game developed by Hovgaard Games. It entered Early Access on March 10, 2023, and reached its full 1.0 release on August 28, 2026. Set in New York City, the game lets players build businesses, manage employees and logistics, purchase property and vehicles, invest money, and develop a personal lifestyle.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Big Ambitions**. This trainer provides quality-of-life controls for Big Ambitions by adjusting personal finances, business resources, inventory, time progression, character condition, and vehicle-related values. It is designed for single-player sandbox sessions where players want to experiment with businesses, logistics networks, real estate, and city development without repeating routine preparation tasks.

**Target Audience:** Players who enjoy business simulations, management sandboxes, life sims, logistics planning, and experimenting with large commercial empires. It is especially useful for testing store layouts, supply chains, staffing plans, real estate strategies, and investment ideas without starting every experiment from scratch.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Personal Funds** — Add money to the player's personal balance for purchases, rent, vehicles, property, investments, and other expenses.
- **Business Finance Control** — Adjust available business funds to support store launches, renovations, payroll, purchasing, and expansion.
- **Freeze Character Condition** — Prevent routine lifestyle deterioration so the player can focus on management, travel, and business operations.
- **Time Progression Control** — Pause or modify the flow of time to make planning, deliveries, store operations, and daily schedules easier to manage.
- **Inventory and Stock Support** — Adjust carried goods or business stock where supported, helping with product testing, restocking, and supply-chain experiments.
- **Vehicle Utility** — Provide vehicle-related quality-of-life options for players who use cars and delivery vehicles while moving products around New York.
- **Business Expansion Sandbox** — Reduce the financial and logistical grind involved in opening shops, hiring staff, furnishing locations, and building larger commercial networks.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat System** — Big Ambitions does not use conventional combat, weapons, or enemy encounters, so the trainer focuses on business, life-simulation, transportation, and economy systems.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use movement-related options while traveling between businesses, warehouses, apartments, dealerships, suppliers, and other locations in New York. Vehicle functions are intended to reduce travel and delivery friction rather than replace the game's normal transportation systems.

### Tools & Inventory

Big Ambitions relies on physical interaction with products, storage areas, shelves, warehouses, and delivery vehicles. Use inventory support for controlled restocking tests or supply-chain experiments, and avoid changing stock while an active delivery, purchase, or logistics task is being processed.

### World & Environment

The trainer's world options are centered on money, time, business operations, property development, and daily character management. Pause time before reorganizing a business, adjusting finances, or planning deliveries, then resume normal progression when the setup is complete.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no conventional combat gameplay in Big Ambitions. The relevant trainer systems are economic and operational, including personal funds, business finances, inventory, time, character condition, vehicles, employees, and logistics.
